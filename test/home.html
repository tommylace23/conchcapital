Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about our platform and profit growth accounts.

Profit growth accounts automatically earn returns through our intelligent growth calculation system. Your funds grow at a rate of 0.71% daily, which compounds to approximately 5% weekly. Growth is calculated automatically every day, and you can track your progress in real-time through your dashboard.

Your Real Wallet contains actual, withdrawable funds that you've deposited. These funds are clearly marked with green borders and can be withdrawn at any time.

Profit Growth accounts are separate investment accounts where you can transfer funds from your Real Wallet. These accounts use dashed blue borders and are designed for growth strategies. Funds in profit growth accounts earn automatic returns but are subject to lock periods.

To deposit funds into ConchCapital, you'll need to purchase cryptocurrency from an external exchange or platform first. Popular options include:

  • Major cryptocurrency exchanges (Binance, Coinbase, Kraken, etc.)
  • Peer-to-peer trading platforms
  • Crypto ATMs (if available in your area)

Once you have cryptocurrency in your external wallet, you can transfer it to your ConchCapital Real Wallet using the deposit feature. We'll provide you with a wallet address where you can send your crypto.

You can make one deposit per week into your profit growth account. This weekly limit helps ensure stable growth calculations and prevents excessive transfers. After making a deposit, you'll need to wait 7 days before you can make another transfer to your profit growth account.

Real Wallet funds can be withdrawn at any time - there are no restrictions on withdrawing from your real wallet balance.

For Profit Growth accounts, funds are locked for 7 days after each deposit to allow for the growth period. Once the lock period ends, you can withdraw funds from your profit growth account back to your Real Wallet, and then withdraw to your external wallet.

We take security seriously. Your real funds are kept separate from profit growth activities and are clearly marked throughout the platform. We use industry-standard security measures to protect your account and transactions.

However, it's important to understand that all investments carry risk. Profit growth accounts are designed for growth strategies, and while we provide transparent calculations and clear distinctions between real and profit growth funds, you should only invest what you can afford to use for growth purposes.

Growth is calculated using compound interest at a rate of 0.71% per day. This means:

  • Each day, your balance grows by 0.71%
  • The growth compounds daily (you earn growth on your previous day's balance plus growth)
  • After 7 days, this compounds to approximately 20% total growth
  • Calculations happen automatically every day

You can use our calculator on this page to see exactly how your funds will grow over time.

When you transfer funds to a profit growth account, they are locked for 7 days. During this period:

  • Your funds earn daily growth at 0.71% per day
  • You cannot withdraw the locked funds until the period ends
  • You can still view your balance and see it growing in real-time
  • After 7 days, the lock automatically releases and funds become available for withdrawal

The lock period ensures stable growth calculations and protects the integrity of the growth system.

We strive to keep our platform transparent. Standard network fees may apply for cryptocurrency transactions (deposits and withdrawals) as these are processed on the blockchain. These fees are determined by the network, not by ConchCapital. There are no hidden fees for using profit growth accounts or for growth calculations.

The minimum deposit amount for profit growth accounts is $0.01. You can start with any amount you're comfortable with. However, we recommend starting with an amount that allows you to see meaningful growth over time.

Profit growth accounts are designed to grow your funds through automatic calculations. The growth rate is fixed at 0.71% daily (5% weekly), and calculations are transparent and automated.

However, as with any investment platform, it's important to understand that all investments carry inherent risks. You should only invest funds that you can afford to use for growth purposes. Always do your own research and invest responsibly.

Getting started is simple:

  1. Create an account - Sign up for free with your email address
  2. Verify your email - Check your inbox and click the verification link
  3. Purchase cryptocurrency - Buy crypto from an external exchange
  4. Deposit to Real Wallet - Transfer your crypto to your ConchCapital Real Wallet
  5. Start Profit Growth - Transfer funds from your Real Wallet to a profit growth account

No credit card is required to create an account. You can explore the platform and use the calculator to see potential returns before making any deposits.

After the 7-day lock period ends:

  • Your funds are automatically unlocked
  • Growth continues at 0.71% daily on your full balance (including all previous growth)
  • You can withdraw funds from your profit growth account back to your Real Wallet
  • You can make another deposit (subject to the weekly limit)
  • All future growth compounds on your total balance

The growth never stops - it continues daily as long as you have funds in your profit growth account.
