1. Capital at risk

All investment carries risk. The value of your holdings may fall as well as rise and you may recover less than you deposited. Past performance is not a reliable indicator of future results.

2. Profit Growth accounts

Profit Growth accounts are managed allocations with published parameters. Growth accrues on the balance recorded against the account and compounds on the schedule shown at the time of allocation.

Stated rates are targets set by ConchCapital. They are applied consistently and audited against the account ledger, but they are not a promise of future performance.

3. Lock periods and liquidity

Funds allocated to a Profit Growth account are locked for seven (7) days. During the lock period the allocation cannot be withdrawn, though growth continues to accrue.

Allocations are limited to one per week. Withdrawal requests are processed within the settlement windows in the Fee Schedule.

4. Market and counterparty risk

Digital assets are volatile and may be subject to liquidity constraints, exchange outages and regulatory change. Where assets are held with custodians or third parties, you are exposed to their credit and operational risk.

5. Currency risk

Accounts are denominated in USD. If you fund or withdraw in another currency or in digital assets, exchange-rate movement may reduce or increase your balance independently of performance.

6. No advice

Nothing on this platform constitutes investment, tax or legal advice. Consider your own circumstances and, where appropriate, take independent professional advice before allocating capital.

7. Operational risk

Systems may be interrupted by maintenance, cyber incidents or events outside our control. Controls are maintained to protect client assets, but no system is entirely free of risk.

8. How we manage risk

  • Segregated records. Client positions are tracked individually against a single platform ledger, reconciled daily.
  • Cold storage custody. Digital assets are held in hardware-secured cold storage rather than on exchanges.
  • Dual control on payouts. Every withdrawal is reviewed and approved before settlement.
  • Two-factor authentication. Available on every account and recommended before your first withdrawal.
  • Full audit trail. Credits, debits, growth and transfers are written to an immutable activity log.

9. Acknowledgement

By funding an account you confirm that you have read and understood this notice and that you are able to bear the loss of some or all of the capital allocated.
