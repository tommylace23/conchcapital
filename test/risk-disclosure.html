Risk Disclosure

Last updated: 22 November, 2025

1. Capital at Risk

All investments carry risk. The value of your holdings may fall as well as rise, and you may receive back less than the amount originally deposited. Past performance is not a reliable indicator of future results.

2. Profit Growth Accounts

Profit Growth accounts are managed portfolio strategies. Their stated growth parameters are targets established by ConchCapital and do not represent actual market transactions, dividends, or interest earned on any specific asset.

Growth accrues on the balance recorded in your account and compounds according to the parameters selected. Stated rates are illustrative targets and are not guaranteed.

3. Lock Periods and Liquidity

Funds transferred into a Profit Growth account are subject to a minimum lock period (currently seven (7) days). During the lock period, those funds cannot be withdrawn.

Deposits into Profit Growth are limited to one (1) per week. Withdrawal requests are processed subject to the settlement timelines published in the Fee Schedule.

4. Market and Counterparty Risk

Cryptoassets are highly volatile and may be subject to liquidity constraints, exchange outages, and regulatory change. Where funds are held in custody or with third-party providers, you are exposed to the credit and operational risk of those parties.

5. Currency Risk

Accounts are denominated in USD. If you fund or withdraw in another currency or in cryptoassets, exchange-rate movements may reduce or increase the value of your balance independently of investment performance.

6. No Advice

Nothing on this platform constitutes investment, tax, or legal advice. You should consider your own circumstances and, where appropriate, obtain independent professional advice before investing.

7. Operational Risk

Systems may be interrupted by maintenance, cyber incidents, or events outside our control. We maintain controls designed to protect client assets, but no system is entirely free from risk.

8. Acknowledgement

By using the platform, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Risk Disclosure and that you are able to bear the loss of some or all of your invested capital.
